## Difficulty: 3/10 — very DIY-friendly

The L300 is a 1980s design — everything's accessible and there's no complex electronics in the way. Experienced Delica owners consistently describe this as a straightforward afternoon job.

## Steps

1. Drain coolant via the drain valve at the bottom of the radiator.
2. Disconnect the upper and lower radiator hoses.
3. Disconnect the overflow/expansion bottle hose.
4. Remove the fan shroud — start by removing the fans from the shroud, which then unbolts from the radiator tanks.
5. Lift out the radiator. It sits on rubber isolator bushings at the bottom — rock it side to side to free it.
6. Reverse the steps to install.

## The extra step for automatic transmission (4FA/T)

Two ATF cooler lines run into one of the radiator tanks. **Pinch or plug these before disconnecting** — have rags ready and a container underneath. This is the messiest part of the job. It adds maybe 20 minutes but isn't mechanically difficult.

## Gotchas

- **The lower radiator hose has an angled/elbow fitting**, not a straight connection — note its orientation before pulling it so you don't guess wrong on reinstall.
- **Core thickness matters:** the diesel JDM Delica radiator core should be at least 38 mm thick. Double-check any replacement before it arrives — some listed "compatible" radiators run thinner.
- **30+ year old hoses and clamps** may be corroded or brittle. Worth having new upper and lower hoses on hand (**MB356382** and **MB356383**) to replace while you're in there rather than fighting the old ones back on.

## Time budget

Realistically 2–3 hours for a first attempt, including draining, bleeding the cooling system afterwards, and cleaning up the inevitable ATF drips.
